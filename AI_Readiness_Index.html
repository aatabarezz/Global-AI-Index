What is the Global AI Index?

The Global AI Index by The Observer ranks 94 countries across 108 indicators from 24 datasets — measuring not who built the best model, but who has the capacity to continuously build, deploy, finance, scale, and benefit from AI over the next decade.

35% Implementation Talent · Infrastructure · Operating Env.
35% Innovation Research · Development

🇺🇸
US leads — dominates talent, infrastructure, R&D, and commercial ecosystem simultaneously. The flywheel of capital → compute → talent keeps spinning.
🇨🇳
China is the only serious challenger — the only country competing across the full AI stack, backed by state-supported industrial acceleration at scale.
🇪🇺
Europe risks falling behind — world-class research and talent, but struggles to convert scientific excellence into frontier companies and commercial scale.

The AI race is no longer about who publishes the best paper. It's about who builds the most complete ecosystem — and increasingly, who controls the electricity, compute, and capital behind it.
